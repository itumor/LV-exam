# Refund Policy

This Refund Policy describes when and how you can obtain a refund for
purchases on Latvian A2 Exam Studio ("the Service").

## 1. B2C 7-day money-back guarantee

If you are a consumer resident in the European Union, the United
Kingdom, or another jurisdiction that grants a statutory cooling-off
right, you may cancel a purchase within **7 days of the original
charge** and receive a full refund, subject to the conditions below.

This 7-day window is offered in addition to (and is consistent with)
the 14-day right of withdrawal under Directive 2011/83/EU. We
expressly grant the 7-day refund window from the date of charge,
which may exceed the EU 14-day window only when the user has not
substantially consumed the service.

### Conditions

- The refund request must be submitted within 7 days of the charge.
- Refunds will be reduced pro-rata for substantial use of the service
  (e.g. more than 3 graded AI scoring runs, more than 5 completed
  exam attempts, or more than 20 unique audio uploads).
- One refund per customer per 12-month period.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method via Stripe.
- Refunds are typically processed within 5 to 10 business days.

### How to request

Email billing@lvexam.example (placeholder) with:

1. Your account email.
2. The Stripe receipt number or charge ID.
3. A brief reason (optional but appreciated).

## 2. Loss of the right of withdrawal

Under Art. 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU you waive the right of
withdrawal once the digital content has been fully supplied with your
prior express consent. At checkout we collect this consent and clearly
state that you are giving up the 14-day statutory window for the
content already consumed. The 7-day money-back policy in section 1
nonetheless applies to the unconsumed remainder of your purchase.

## 3. Subscriptions

For monthly or annual subscriptions:

- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- We do not refund partial months unless required by mandatory local
  law (e.g. Latvian Consumer Rights Protection Law if the service was
  defective).
- Annual plans are eligible for the 7-day window only on the initial
  purchase, not on automatic renewals.

## 4. Institutional / B2B contracts

Refunds, credit notes, and termination for institutional contracts
(schools, employers, agencies) are governed by the master purchase
agreement signed at the time of the order. The 7-day B2C guarantee
does **not** apply to B2B contracts.

## 5. Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank or card scheme without first
contacting us, we may suspend your account pending resolution of the
chargeback. We encourage you to contact billing@lvexam.example first
so we can resolve issues quickly.

## 6. Faulty service

If the Service is materially defective (e.g. you paid for AI scoring
but our AI provider was unavailable for more than 24 hours and no
substitute was offered), you are entitled to a full refund or credit
for the affected period, in addition to the rights in section 1.

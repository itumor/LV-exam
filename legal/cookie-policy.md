# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Latvian A2 Exam Studio uses cookies
and similar technologies (collectively "cookies") on the Service.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. We also use
related browser storage such as `localStorage` and `sessionStorage`.
For brevity we refer to all of these as "cookies" in this policy.

## 2. Cookies we use

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `session` | Strictly necessary | Authenticated session token | Session |
| `csrf_token` | Strictly necessary | Cross-site request forgery protection | Session |
| `cookies_acknowledged` | Strictly necessary | Records that you have seen the cookie banner | Until cleared (localStorage) |
| `attempt_in_progress` | Functional | Resumes an in-flight exam attempt after a refresh | Session (localStorage) |
| `lvexam.locale` | Functional | Stores your interface language preference | 12 months (localStorage) |

Strictly necessary cookies are required to deliver the Service and do
not require consent under the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC) and
GDPR Recital 30.

## 3. What we do NOT use

At the time of this policy we do not set advertising, retargeting, or
cross-site tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics or
similar third-party analytics scripts on the public Service.

If this changes, we will update this policy and re-prompt for consent
where required.

## 4. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies via your browser settings. Blocking
strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from logging in or
completing an exam attempt.

To re-show the cookie banner, clear the `cookies_acknowledged` entry
in your browser's localStorage and reload the page.
